# Subscriptions And Commerce

Read this only for the `subscriptions-commerce` mode.

1. Classify each paid item, its user-visible benefit, store purchase path, entitlement source, and any claimed policy exception; verify current store rules before relying on an exception.
2. Reconcile product identifiers, catalog/offering state, app copy, backend or entitlement service, and reviewer/test access.
3. Verify material purchase terms before conversion: price/period, renewal, trial/offer limits, cancellation, included benefits, usage limits, and failure/refund behavior where relevant.
4. Exercise purchase, cancellation, interruption, restore/recovery, expiration, upgrade/downgrade, unavailable-product, and backend-validation paths appropriate to the product.
5. Keep paid access, account deletion, privacy controls, and offline/degraded behavior coherent; do not hide a required user control behind an entitlement.

Do not infer cross-platform commerce behavior from one store. Escalate an unresolved billing exception, catalog mismatch, or reviewer-access gap.
