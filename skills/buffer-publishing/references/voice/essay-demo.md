# Essay: I couldn't demo my own app (Josh, written for X, 2026-10)

Voice sample, verbatim. Contains the public TestFlight link.

For most of last year, I couldn't demo my own app.
Praize started as a Bible app. A good one, I thought... reading plans, translations, and tucked inside it, a feature where you could hear someone's prayer request and record a prayer for them in your own voice. When I showed it to people, I'd watch the same thing happen every time. Their eyes moved across the screens. Too many buttons. Polite nodding at the reading plans. Scrolling in seconds past things that took me weeks.
Eventually I stopped demoing and just said it: it's an audio-only prayer app.
And people got it instantly. Not "interesting" or "what's the market." They'd say oh, yeah, that would be good, in the tone you use for something you already knew and were waiting for someone to say out loud. Nobody has ever needed the idea explained to them. Someone hears your request. They stop, they say your name, and they pray for you, out loud. Not "thoughts and prayers." Not "praying 🙏" typed in three seconds. A voice. Deliberate. Like a hand on your shoulder. Someone asking God for you.
I've spent the six months since making the app as simple as the sentence. That has mostly meant removing things. The Bible reader went. Then everything that made it resemble a normal social app: the feed, the likes, the follower counts, the pull to post about yourself. None of it was evil. It was just noise around a signal people could already hear perfectly when it was three words long.
Here's what I didn't expect: I didn't actually make five decisions. I made one, and then refused to contradict it four times. Once the unit of the app is one person praying aloud for another, the rest falls out on its own. A feed would make prayers compete. Likes would make them performances. Follower counts would turn pray-ers into creators. Every familiar social mechanic had an obvious justification, and every one of them quietly worked against the only thing the app was for.
People praying for each other. That's it.
Which sounds like a small thing to build. It is. But I've come to believe the small thing is about to become the scarce thing.
Here's what I mean. For the internet's whole life, content has been the unit of value, and content was expensive. Writing something good took hours. Making something beautiful took skill. Polish was proof of effort. When you saw quality, you knew a person had cared. AI ends that. Not by making content worse, but by making it infinite. The essay, the encouragement, the perfectly worded sympathy message, the four-minute devotional. All of it will be instant, fluent, and free. And when something becomes free, the value doesn't disappear. It moves.
It moves to the one thing generation can't cheapen: the fact that a person actually did something. Not the words, the event. Anyone will be able to produce a beautiful message. What can't be produced is someone stopped what they were doing for you. The future internet won't be short on expression. It will be short on evidence that anyone meant it. Provenance, intention, cost. The things quality used to prove and no longer does.
And here's the strange thing about prayer: it might be the purest case there is. A prayer has no deliverable. Nothing to download, nothing to consume, no output that could be judged well-made or badly made. Strip everything away and what remains is only the event. A person, on a given evening, chose to carry your name to God. AI can generate everything about a prayer except the fact of it. Which means the thing I stripped my app down to isn't just resistant to what's coming. It's almost the definition of what will be left.
Praize is empty right now. No prayers yet, and I'm not going to fake them, for reasons this whole essay should make obvious. So the first people in will either bring the first requests or pray the first prayers. If you're carrying something, you might be the first name spoken in there. And if you want to be the person who stops for someone else, there will be a request waiting soon enough. The door is here: https://testflight.apple.com/join/Ts93uUuq.
An audio-only prayer app. That's the whole thing.
