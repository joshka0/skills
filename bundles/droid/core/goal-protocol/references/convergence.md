# Goal convergence

Read only for a new long-horizon goal or a goal review, stress test, pivot, or compacting pass.

1. Treat the stated goal as a hypothesis. Capture the horizon, desired state, current facts, constraints, anti-goals, assumptions, stakes, and what must not happen.
2. Generate genuinely different candidate goals, then compare alignment, leverage, feasibility, measurability, reversibility, information value, and opportunity cost. Scores expose tradeoffs; they do not decide them.
3. Cross-examine serious candidates for hidden assumptions, failure paths, and the smallest experiment that could change the decision. Preserve material dissent rather than averaging it away.
4. Before locking, run a red team with three substantive attacks (or a reasoned none-found), its strongest objection, and the cheapest invalidating test. A blocking verdict returns to convergence unless the user overrides it.
5. Lock only when `GOAL.md` has a clear outcome, horizon, metric and leading indicator, anti-goals, constraints, assumptions, preserved dissent, first milestone/slice, review cadence, and pivot or exit trigger.

For maintenance, compare actual with expected progress, then recommend continue, adjust, narrow, experiment, pause, pivot, or archive. A pivot preserves reusable learning and creates a new version rather than rewriting the prior decision.
