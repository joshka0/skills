# Out-of-scope record

Read only after a maintainer approves rejection of an enhancement and wants durable decision memory.

1. Record one concept, not one issue, at `.out-of-scope/<concept>.md` or the repository's configured equivalent. Include the decision, durable rationale, and linked prior requests.
2. Distinguish a scope/philosophy/architecture decision from a temporary capacity deferral. Only the former belongs here.
3. During later triage, surface a concept match for confirmation rather than silently closing the new request.
4. When the maintainer reconsiders, mark the prior record superseded or update it according to repository convention; do not erase the decision history by default.

Preview the tracker comment and record update before writing or closing anything public.
