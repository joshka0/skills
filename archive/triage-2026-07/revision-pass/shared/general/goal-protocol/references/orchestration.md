# Goal orchestration

Read only when approved goal and dossier artifacts need tickets, waves, execution, status, or replanning.

1. Refuse to decompose a missing, pending, or revised parent gate; route it to `converge` or `blueprint` first.
2. Build tickets that are one-agent/session-sized, self-contained, parent-traced, verifiable, scoped with non-goals, and independent of conversation memory.
3. Compare cut lines before selecting them. Prefer vertical behavior slices; make a genuine shared foundation an explicit early ticket.
4. Build a dependency DAG and file-disjoint waves with exit criteria. End with an integration and verification wave; preserve completed ticket IDs when replanning.
5. Red-team the breakdown for hidden dependencies, false parallelism, unverifiable work, deferred integration, and orphan work before execution.
6. Dispatch only approved-wave tickets with the goal root, ledger, relevant IDs, allowed scope, done criteria, escalation triggers, and report destination. The supervisor integrates reports and ledger state.

Stop on an unapproved file, invariant conflict, dossier mismatch, schema/API/dependency expansion, or unavailable verification. Do not let a ticket improvise past an escalation trigger.
