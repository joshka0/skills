# Accessibility, Rating, And Regulated Scope

Read this only for the `accessibility-rating-regulated` mode.

1. Test critical user journeys with relevant assistive technology, increased text, contrast/dark themes, reduced motion, non-color status cues, and non-audio alternatives.
2. Reconcile age/content rating and target-audience declarations with actual AI, UGC, chat, mature content, purchases, marketing assets, and in-app controls.
3. Verify minors cannot reach age-restricted features without the required gate, moderation, and reporting controls.
4. Identify any regulated category or restricted capability—health, finance, government, network/VPN, accessibility services, background access, gambling, dating, or high-stakes AI—and assign legal/product ownership for eligibility evidence.
5. Keep store labels, privacy disclosures, screenshots, and product behavior consistent; do not claim accessibility or regulatory readiness without the tested build and current requirements.

Stop the release decision when a regulated claim, audience/rating answer, or restricted capability lacks evidence or a qualified owner.
